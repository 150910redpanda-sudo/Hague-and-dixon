01 — Property

Conveyancing

Your home is usually your largest investment, and you may only move a handful of times in your life. Our CQS-accredited conveyancing team makes buying, selling and remortgaging as smooth and stress-free as possible — keeping you informed at every stage, whether the property is residential or commercial.

  • Residential sales & purchases
  • Remortgages
  • Commercial property
  • Transfers of equity
  • New-build purchases
  • Leasehold & freehold

02 — Family

Family Law & Children

Family breakdown can be traumatic, and advice at an early stage helps reduce both confusion and worry. Our solicitors are members of Resolution and trained in collaborative family law, balancing the legal process with genuine understanding of the personal and emotional difficulties involved.

  • Divorce & separation
  • Financial matters on divorce
  • Children & arrangements
  • Cohabitation disputes
  • Collaborative family law
  • Pre-nuptial agreements

Wills, Probate & Estates

Many people assume their estate will simply pass to their next of kin — but without a Will, the outcome may be very different from your wishes. We help you plan ahead with care, and support families through the administration of an estate with sensitivity and efficiency.

  • Administration of estates
  • Probate
  • Lasting Powers of Attorney
  • Court of Protection
  • Deputyship applications
