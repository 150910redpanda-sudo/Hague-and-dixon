— Private client

Lasting Powers of Attorney

A Lasting Power of Attorney appoints someone you trust to make decisions on your behalf if you're ever unable to — through illness, an accident or in later life. We strongly recommend putting one in place while you have the capacity to do so, rather than leaving your family to apply to the Court of Protection.

  • Property & Financial Affairs LPA
  • Health & Welfare LPA
  • LPAs for individuals and business owners
  • Registration with the Office of the Public Guardian
