Qualifications & regulation

Every solicitor at Hague & Dixon LLP is admitted as a Solicitor of the Senior Courts of England & Wales and practises through the firm, which is authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority under firm number 629017. Our paralegals and legal assistants work on client matters under the supervision of a solicitor of the firm.

You can check the practising status of any individual solicitor, and of the firm itself, on the Solicitors Regulation Authority's public register. If you would like details of the qualifications and experience of the person who will handle your matter, please just ask — we will tell you before you instruct us.
