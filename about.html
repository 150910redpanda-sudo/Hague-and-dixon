Welcome

Quality legal advice, at a fair price, with a personal touch.

Hague & Dixon LLP offers a full range of legal services, with particular strength in conveyancing, family law, business, wills and probate, and employment. Care is taken to deliver a professional service in an efficient way — an approach that has won us a long-standing and loyal client base.

That loyalty reaches well beyond our local community. We are proud to act for clients nationally and internationally, while remaining the kind of firm where you will always speak to a person who knows your name and your matter.

Our story

From a single York office to a Yorkshire institution.

1903

The beginning

Hague & Dixon opens its doors in York, providing legal advice to the people and businesses of the city. The values set out then — diligence, fairness and genuine care — remain the foundation of the firm today.

20th c.

Growing with Yorkshire

As generations of clients return — many for decades, and across families — the firm expands its reach into the surrounding towns and villages, opening offices that put trusted advice within easy reach of the communities it serves.

Today

Three offices, one standard

Now a Limited Liability Partnership regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, Hague & Dixon LLP advises clients across Yorkshire, England, Europe and beyond from offices in York, Stamford Bridge and Pickering — holding Lexcel and CQS accreditation for quality of service.

What sets us apart
