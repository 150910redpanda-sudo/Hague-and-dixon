Our story

A name Yorkshire has trusted for four generations.

Founded in York at the turn of the last century, Hague & Dixon has grown alongside the county it serves — yet our principle has never changed. Quality legal advice, at fair prices, delivered with the personal touch that larger firms forget.

Today we advise clients not only across Yorkshire but throughout England, Europe and beyond, from three offices rooted firmly in the communities we know best.

  • Authorised & regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority
  • Lexcel and CQS accredited for quality and conveyancing
  • A long-standing, loyal client base — local and international
