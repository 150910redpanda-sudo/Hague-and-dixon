Why Hague & Dixon

A good practice to grow in.

We strive to deliver quality legal advice and an excellent level of client service, and that depends entirely on the people who work here. It is why we pride ourselves on our staff retention and on nurturing our employees rather than simply setting them targets.

We offer a good work/life balance with a competitive salary, without an onerous emphasis on target achievement. For the right candidates there is real scope for progression within the firm.

  • Competitive salary and a genuine work/life balance
  • No onerous emphasis on billing targets
  • Real scope for progression within the firm
  • Supervision and support from experienced directors
  • Three Yorkshire offices — York, Stamford Bridge and Pickering

Speculative applications welcome

We do not have a specific role advertised at the moment. We are, however, always pleased to hear from talented people who share our commitment to client care, and we will consider a speculative application on its merits.

Whether you are a solicitor, a chartered legal executive, a paralegal or an experienced support professional, do get in touch and tell us what you are looking for.

Training and early-career enquiries. We are a small firm and do not run a formal graduate scheme, so we cannot promise a training contract or an apprenticeship at any given time. 02 — The fit

What We Look For

Legal knowledge matters, but it is not the whole picture. Our clients stay with us because of how they are treated, so we look for people who are organised, efficient, communicate well and have a strong eye for detail.

01

Organised & efficient

You keep on top of a caseload, meet deadlines without being chased, and know what needs doing next.

02

A good communicator

You explain things in plain English, keep clients informed, and are comfortable picking up the phone.

03

A strong eye for detail

You check your work. In legal practice small errors become expensive ones, and accuracy is the whole job.

04 — Your data

Your Application & Your Data

Anything you send us as part of an application is handled in line with our privacy policy. In short:

  • What we use it for — assessing your suitability for a role at the firm, and nothing else.
  • Who sees it — the directors involved in recruitment, and no one outside the firm.
  • Your rights — you can ask us at any time what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.

Please do not send confidential information belonging to a current or former employer with your application. Read our privacy policy for the full detail on how we handle personal data, including how to make a request or a complaint.

Equal opportunities

We appoint on merit, and we want applications from people of every background.

Hague & Dixon LLP does not discriminate on the grounds of age, disability, gender reassignment, marriage or civil partnership, pregnancy or maternity, race, religion or belief, sex, or sexual orientation. If you need an adjustment at any stage of the process — to the application itself, to an interview, or to the working arrangements of a role — tell us and we will do what we reasonably can to accommodate it.
