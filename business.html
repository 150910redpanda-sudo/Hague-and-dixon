What we handle

From formation to exit — and everything between.

01

Business Sales & Acquisitions

Whether you are buying a business, selling one, or merging with another — this is a significant transaction that demands careful legal work. We advise on both share sales and asset sales, conduct due diligence, draft and negotiate the transaction documents, and guide you through to completion. We understand that commercial transactions often move quickly, and we match that pace.

  • Share sales and purchases
  • Asset sales and purchases
  • Business due diligence
  • Warranties, indemnities and disclosure
  • Management buy-ins (MBI) and management buy-outs (MBO)
  • Earn-out structures
  • Post-completion matters
02

Commercial Property

Buying or leasing business premises is one of the most significant commitments a business makes. We handle the full range of commercial property transactions, from small office leases to major commercial purchases, acting for both owners and occupiers. We work in close collaboration with our conveyancing team on transactions that combine residential and commercial elements.

  • Commercial property purchases and sales
  • Commercial leases — landlord and tenant
  • Lease assignments and sub-lettings
  • Lease renewals and surrenders
  • Licences to occupy
  • Development and investment transactions
  • Agricultural and rural land
03

Company & Partnership Structures

Getting the legal structure of your business right at the outset can save considerable time and expense later. We advise on the most appropriate vehicle for your business, prepare the necessary constitutional documents and ensure the arrangements between the owners are clear and properly recorded.

  • Company formation and corporate governance
  • Shareholders' agreements
  • Partnership agreements (general and limited)
  • LLP agreements
  • Director's service agreements
  • Changes to share capital and ownership structure
  • Joint ventures
04

Venture Capital & Investment

For growing businesses seeking investment, or investors looking to deploy capital, we advise on the legal framework for equity investment — from seed rounds to larger institutional transactions. We have experience with both EIS and SEIS-qualifying investments, and understand the importance of speed and pragmatism in early-stage transactions.

  • Seed and angel investment rounds
  • EIS and SEIS structuring
  • Term sheet and investment agreement negotiation
  • Convertible loan notes
  • Investor shareholder agreements
05

Intellectual Property

Your brand, your processes and your content may be among the most valuable assets your business holds — even if they do not appear on a balance sheet. We advise on protecting and commercialising intellectual property rights, and on resolving disputes where those rights are infringed.

  • IP assignment agreements
  • Licensing of IP (trade marks, copyright, patents)
  • Confidentiality and non-disclosure agreements
  • Technology and software agreements
  • IP due diligence on business acquisitions
06

Commercial Contracts & Compliance

Well-drafted contracts protect your business when things go wrong — and they often do. We draft, review and negotiate commercial contracts of all kinds, advise on compliance requirements, and help resolve disputes before they reach litigation. Prevention is always better — and less expensive — than a cure.

  • Terms and conditions of sale and purchase
  • Supply and distribution agreements
  • Agency and franchise agreements
  • Data protection and GDPR compliance advice
  • Commercial dispute resolution
  • Letters of claim and pre-action correspondence
