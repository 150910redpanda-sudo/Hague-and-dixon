Our approach

Resolution. Collaboration. Results.

Our family solicitors are members of Resolution, a national organisation of family law professionals committed to a non-confrontational approach to resolving family issues. We are trained in collaborative family law — a process that keeps you and your former partner in control of the outcome, with the aim of avoiding the stress and cost of court proceedings wherever possible.

That does not mean we are not firm advocates when you need us to be. Where matters are contested, we will represent your interests clearly and effectively. Our goal is always the best outcome for you and your family.

  • Members of Resolution — the family law professional body
  • Trained in collaborative family law
  • Experienced in contested court proceedings
  • Sensitive to the personal and emotional dimensions
  • Advice given at an early stage reduces worry and cost

What we handle

Every aspect of family law.

01

Divorce & Separation

Since the introduction of no-fault divorce in 2022, ending a marriage is legally more straightforward — but it remains a significant life event, and the process of separating finances and arrangements for children requires careful legal advice. We guide you through the divorce procedure itself and help you understand what comes next.

  • Sole and joint applications for divorce
  • Judicial separation
  • Dissolution of civil partnerships
  • Advice on timing and procedure
  • Liaison with your spouse's solicitor
02

Financial Matters on Divorce

Dividing assets on separation is often the most complicated and contested part of the process. We advise you on what a fair settlement looks like, negotiate with the other side where possible, and — if agreement cannot be reached — represent you in court. We also draft Consent Orders to make any agreed settlement legally binding.

  • Financial disclosure & asset valuation
  • Negotiated financial settlements
  • Consent Orders (binding financial agreements)
  • Contested financial proceedings in court
  • Pension sharing & pension attachment orders
  • Maintenance & periodical payments
  • Clean break orders
03

Children & Child Arrangements

When parents separate, ensuring the right arrangements for their children is the most important — and often the most sensitive — issue. We always put the welfare of the children first, encouraging agreement between parents wherever possible. Where court proceedings are necessary, we represent you clearly and with care.

  • Child Arrangements Orders (where the child lives and spends time)
  • Specific Issue Orders (schools, medical treatment, holidays abroad)
  • Prohibited Steps Orders
  • Parental Responsibility
  • Relocation applications (within England & Wales and internationally)
  • Grandparents' rights
04

Collaborative Family Law

Collaborative family law is a process in which you, your former partner, and your respective solicitors all meet together to resolve matters by agreement — without going to court. It is particularly effective where both parties want to maintain a constructive relationship, especially where children are involved. Our solicitors are trained collaborative practitioners.

The collaborative process typically costs less than contested court proceedings, gives both parties more control over the outcome, and tends to produce agreements that both sides feel ownership of — and are therefore more likely to honour.

  • Four-way meetings with both solicitors present
  • Agreement-based resolution without court
  • Suitable for both financial and children matters
  • Can be used alongside mediation
05

Cohabitation

Contrary to popular belief, there is no such thing as a "common law spouse" in English law. Unmarried couples who separate have very limited automatic rights to each other's property, regardless of how long they have lived together. We advise cohabiting couples on how to protect themselves — and those who are separating on what remedies are available.

  • Cohabitation agreements (living together agreements)
  • Property disputes on separation (TOLATA claims)
  • Claims for children's financial provision
  • Declarations of beneficial interest
06

Pre-Nuptial Agreements

A pre-nuptial agreement sets out how assets would be divided if a marriage or civil partnership were to break down. While not strictly legally binding, a well-drafted agreement gives a court a strong starting point and significantly reduces the risk of costly litigation. We advise both parties on pre-nuptial (and post-nuptial) agreements.

  • Pre-nuptial agreements
  • Post-nuptial agreements
  • Advice to both parties (each taking separate advice)
  • Suitable for both first marriages and subsequent relationships
07

Domestic Violence & Non-Molestation Orders

If you are experiencing domestic violence or harassment, you can apply to the court for a Non-Molestation Order or an Occupation Order to protect yourself and any children. These orders can be obtained quickly in urgent cases. We treat every enquiry with the utmost sensitivity and confidentiality, and will act swiftly to secure the protection you need.

  • Non-Molestation Orders
  • Occupation Orders (requiring a person to leave the family home)
  • Emergency applications where urgent protection is needed
  • Advice on the criminal and civil routes available
  • Liaison with support services and refuges where appropriate
