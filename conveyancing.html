What we handle

Every type of property transaction.

From a first flat purchase to a complex commercial acquisition, our conveyancing team has the experience to guide you through every stage.

01

Buying a Home

Whether you are buying your first home or your fifth, the legal process can feel daunting. We take ownership of your transaction from the moment you instruct us, checking the title, raising enquiries, reporting to you on the contract, managing searches, and coordinating exchange and completion. We report to you in plain English — not in legal jargon.

  • Freehold & leasehold purchases
  • First-time buyer guidance
  • New-build properties
  • Shared Ownership & Help to Buy
  • Right to Buy
  • Gifted deposit & family assistance
  • Auction purchases
02

Selling a Home

A swift, well-prepared sale starts with a solicitor who acts early. We prepare your contract pack as soon as you instruct us, so buyers and their solicitors are not kept waiting. We handle all replies to enquiries, discharge your existing mortgage and ensure the proceeds reach you on the day of completion — with a full account of how they were applied.

  • Freehold & leasehold sales
  • Sale alongside a related purchase (chain)
  • Probate sales
  • Matrimonial & court order sales
  • Transfers of equity (part sale, adding/removing a name)
03

Remortgaging

When you remortgage, your new lender will require legal work to be carried out on the property. We can often act for both you and the new lender simultaneously, reducing cost and keeping the process as simple as possible. Most straightforward remortgages complete within 4–6 weeks of instruction.

  • Acting for borrower and lender
  • Change of mortgage terms
  • Further advance
  • Equity release

Commercial Property

Our experience extends well beyond the residential market. We advise business owners, investors and developers on the purchase, sale and lease of commercial premises throughout England & Wales — from small business units to larger commercial estates. Commercial transactions are quoted individually.

Leasehold property comes with its own set of legal considerations — service charges, ground rent, management company obligations, and lease length. What to expect

The residential conveyancing process, step by step.

Every transaction is different, but here is a clear guide to what happens — and when — on a standard residential purchase.

01

Letter of Engagement

We will send you out letter of engagement. This will guide you through our compliance procedures, tell you what we will do, what we won't do, thereby defining the service we will provide and it gives you the important information needed to proceed — such as costs and timeframes.

02

Instruction & ID checks

We must ask for ID to establish who you are, where you live and when you are selling, to establish your connection with the property. When you are purchasing, we must also get you to provide evidence of your wealth, how you created your wealth and where from that wealth the funds for the transaction will come from. This is what we call compliance.

03

Legal pack

If you are selling, we will, with your help, put the legal pack together. If you are purchasing, we will receive, review and advise you on the legal pack.

04

Searches

If you are purchasing, we will order and advise you on the results of your conveyancing searches. We will usually order searches with the local authority, water and drainage authority, and an environmental search provider. These reveal planning history, drainage connections, flood risk and other factors that may affect the property or your decision to buy.

05

Enquiries

If you are selling we may receive some precontract enquiries. With your help, we will reply to them. If you are purchasing, we may advise you that it is necessary to raise some pre-contract enquiries. You may instruct us to raise some pre-contract enquires of your own. They should be specific and relevant to your decision to buy.

06

Reporting to you

When you are buying, once we have all the information, we prepare a clear written report for you. We will explain what the documents show, raise any concerns and answer your questions — including explaining the financial obligations at completion.

07

Exchange of contracts

Both parties sign the contract and we exchange, locking in the agreed price and completion date. You pay your deposit at this stage. The transaction is now legally binding.

08

Completion

On the agreed date, we send the purchase funds, receive the keys, and the property is yours. We then register your ownership at HM Land Registry and submit your Stamp Duty Land Tax return. You will receive a copy of your registered title once this is complete.
