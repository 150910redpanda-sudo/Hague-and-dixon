How this website uses cookies, and how you can control them.

Notice

Cookies on this website

We use cookies to ensure that we give you the best experience on our website. If you continue to use this site we will assume that you are happy with it.

This website does not currently use tracking or advertising cookies. We may use functional cookies that are strictly necessary for the website to operate — for example, to remember that you have already seen our cookie notice. No personal data is collected by this website beyond what you voluntarily submit through our contact form.

What

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website saves on your computer or mobile device when you visit it. It allows the website to remember your actions and preferences over a period of time, so you don't have to re-enter them every time you visit or move between pages.

Control

Controlling cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings — including blocking or deleting cookies already stored on your device. Because cookies allow you to take advantage of some of our website's features, we recommend that you leave cookies enabled. Details on how to manage cookies can usually be found in your browser's "Help", "Tools" or "Settings" menu.
