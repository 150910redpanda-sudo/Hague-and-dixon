How this website uses cookies, and how you can control them.

Notice

Cookies on this website

This website does not currently set any analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The only thing we store on your device is a single record of the cookie choice you make below, so that we do not have to ask you again on every page.

If we introduce analytics in future, it will only run for visitors who have accepted it here. Nothing non-essential is loaded before you opt in.

This website collects personal data only where you choose to submit it — through the enquiry form on our Contact page or the form on our Client Feedback page. How we handle that data is set out in our Privacy Notice.

Manage your cookie choices

What

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website saves on your computer or mobile device when you visit it. It allows the website to remember your actions and preferences over a period of time, so you don't have to re-enter them every time you visit or move between pages.

Control

Controlling cookies

Alongside the preferences above, most web browsers also allow you to control cookies through their settings — including blocking or deleting cookies already stored on your device. Because cookies allow you to take advantage of some of our website's features, we recommend that you leave essential cookies enabled. Details on how to manage cookies can usually be found in your browser's "Help", "Tools" or "Settings" menu.
